About Overlap

Offshore cost.
Onshore working hours.

Hiring senior engineering talent in the US is expensive. Hiring offshore usually means sending a question at 4 PM and getting an answer the next morning — every single time. Most teams end up choosing which problem they'd rather have.

Overlap removes the trade. Our team is based in Lahore, Pakistan, which keeps your build economics sane — and we run a shifted schedule that lands squarely inside the US business day, so standups, reviews, and blockers get handled live instead of in a 12-hour relay.

Built by senior engineers

Led by developers with 16+ years shipping production systems — not a layer of account managers between you and the build.

Real-time collaboration

Six hours of daily overlap with US business hours. Same standup, same Slack thread, same day.

Design and engineering, together

Interface design and the system behind it are handled by one team, so nothing gets lost in handoff.

What we build

The work that usually
needs a senior team.

Not brochure sites and template installs. Overlap takes on systems with real architectural weight — custom logic, live data, and integrations that have to hold up in production.

Multi-tenant SaaS platforms

Tenant isolation, role-based access control, subscription billing, and admin tooling — architected so adding your hundredth customer doesn't mean rewriting the foundation.

APIs & systems integration

RESTful APIs that sync cleanly with the architecture you already run — POS systems, ERPs, payment processors, and legacy internal services that were never built to talk to anything.

AI features, in production

Real-time AI built into the product rather than bolted beside it — streaming responses, retrieval over your own data, and background pipelines on Next.js and PostgreSQL.

Product & UI/UX design

Interface design, design systems, and full product flows — drawn by designers who sit with the engineers building them, so what ships matches what was designed.
